Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 23, 2026

Eriana Investments LLC ("we," "us," or "our") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, share, and protect information when you visit our website or submit a form to us regarding a potential surplus funds claim.

Information We Collect

When you submit our contact or eligibility form, we may collect your name, phone number, email address, property state and county, and details you provide about your situation.

How We Use Your Information

We use the information you provide to review your potential surplus funds claim, contact you about your case, and, where you have consented, to send you calls or text messages related to your claim.

SMS and Text Messaging Consent

If you check the consent box on our form, you agree to receive calls and text messages from Eriana Investments LLC at the phone number you provide, including through an automatic telephone dialing system and/or prerecorded or artificial voice, regarding your potential surplus funds claim.

Message and data rates may apply. Message frequency varies. You may opt out of text messages at any time by replying STOP, or request help by replying HELP. Consent to receive text messages is not a condition of any purchase or service.

We do not sell or share your mobile phone number or SMS opt-in data with third parties for their own marketing purposes. Your phone number is used solely for communications related to your surplus funds case with Eriana Investments LLC.

How We Share Information

We may share your information with county or court offices, title or escrow professionals, and service providers who assist us in researching, verifying, and filing your claim. We do not sell your personal information.

Data Retention

We retain your information for as long as necessary to process your claim and comply with legal, accounting, or reporting obligations.

Your Choices

You may opt out of text messages at any time by replying STOP. You may contact us at any time to request that we update, correct, or delete your information, subject to any legal recordkeeping requirements.

Security

We use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards to protect the information you provide to us.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
